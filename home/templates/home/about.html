A little about us

About Walkies & Whiskers

Walkies & Whiskers is a local pet care business created to make arranging reliable care feel simpler, warmer and more personal.

We understand that pets are not simply another item on a household schedule. They each have their own personality, routine, preferences and care needs.

Our website gives local pet owners one straightforward place to browse services, create detailed pet profiles, arrange bookings and complete secure online payments.

Where it began

Meet our founder

Walkies & Whiskers was founded by Claire Rossa, a lifelong animal lover who wanted to make dependable pet care easier for busy owners to arrange.

Claire recognised that organising pet care could quickly become a tangle of phone calls, handwritten notes and missing information. She wanted to create a service where an owner's instructions remained clear and every pet could be treated as an individual.

Her aim was simple: to create a friendly local business that owners could trust and a booking experience that did not add more stress to an already busy day.

What matters to us

Care shaped around real pets

Everything we do is guided by three simple values.

Personal care

Every pet is different. Profiles help owners record useful information about temperament, feeding, medical needs and everyday routines.

Clear communication

Booking details, prices and confirmation messages are kept clear so that owners always understand what has been arranged.

Reliable support

Our services are designed to provide dependable help when work, appointments, travel or everyday life make pet care more difficult to manage alone.
